Meter Dost privacy policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

In short

Meter Dost keeps your meters, readings and bill numbers on your phone. There is no Meter Dost server, and we never receive them. The free version shows Google ads, and asks for your consent first where the law requires it.

What stays on your phone

Meter names, company, protected status, sanctioned load, reference or consumer numbers, readings, billing months, reminder settings and any bill you keep are stored only on your phone. Android backup may keep a copy in your own Google account, under your Google settings.

The camera is used only to read the meter. Each frame is read on the phone and then discarded. No photo is saved or sent anywhere.

Your bill

When you open your bill, Meter Dost shows the electricity or gas company's own website inside the app and types your saved number into that page on your phone. The company's website and its own privacy terms apply to that page. For gas bills you type the website's security code yourself. A bill you download or keep is saved on your phone only.

Ads

The free version shows a small banner from Google AdMob on the History and More screens. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your internet address and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where consent is required, Google's consent form asks you first, and you can change your choice from More, Ad privacy choices. Read how Google uses data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Meter Dost Pro shows no ads.

Purchases

Meter Dost Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the app only learns that Pro is owned.

Notifications

Reading reminders and line warnings are made on your phone. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.

Children

Meter Dost is made for adults who pay household bills. It is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

In the app, open More, then Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also removes everything it stored. Your advertising ID can be reset or deleted in Android settings.
