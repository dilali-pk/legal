Meter Dost terms of service

Effective date: 8 November 2026

Estimates, not bills

Meter Dost counts units from the readings you save and estimates your bill from the published NEPRA and OGRA rates. Your company's bill is the only amount you owe. Taxes, fuel price adjustment and other charges are not included, and gas amounts use an average heat value, so your bill can differ.

Not an official app

Meter Dost is not affiliated with any electricity or gas company, NEPRA, OGRA or the Government of Pakistan. Bills open from the companies' own websites, which they run and are responsible for.

Meter Dost Pro

Pro is a one time purchase through Google Play that removes ads and unlocks more meters and export. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. Pro stays with your Google account when you reinstall.

Your responsibility

Read meters safely and only where you are allowed to. Check readings before relying on them; the camera can misread a digit, and you can edit any reading.

The app is provided as is. To the extent the law allows, the developer is not liable for losses from estimates, warnings, missed reminders or third party websites. Nothing here limits rights you have under the law of Pakistan.
