Peek QR privacy policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Peek QR is an Android app made by Dilshad Ali ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information on your phone. In short: your scans, the codes you create and your history stay on your phone. We have no account system and no server that receives your scans.

What stays on your phone

  • Camera. The camera is used only while the scan screen is open, to read codes. Camera frames are processed on your phone and are never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Images you choose to scan. When you scan from an image, Android's photo picker gives the app only the image you pick. It is read on your phone and not kept.
  • Scan and create history. What you scan and the codes you create are saved in the app on your phone so you can find them again. History is not included in cloud backups or device transfers. You can delete one item or clear all history in the app at any time, and uninstalling the app removes it.
  • Wi-Fi passwords. A Wi-Fi password from a scanned code is stored encrypted on your phone with a key held by Android, and is never sent anywhere.
  • Contacts you choose. When you pick a contact to make a contact code, Android's contact picker gives the app only the contact you choose. The app never reads your address book.
  • Link checks. Warnings about links (shortened links, unencrypted http, unusual characters in a web address, raw IP addresses) are worked out on your phone. Scanned links are not sent to us or to any checking service.

Advertising

Peek QR is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Before ads load, regions that require it show a consent message (Google's User Messaging Platform), and you can change your choice any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, general app interaction and diagnostic information. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Google's policy on how it uses information from apps that use its services (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience. The app does not use your scans or your history to choose ads.

The scanning library

Peek QR reads codes with Google's ML Kit barcode scanning library, which runs on your phone. The images and the content of the codes you scan are processed on the phone and are not sent anywhere. To diagnose problems and measure usage of the library, ML Kit may send Google device information (such as manufacturer, model and Android version), app information (package name and version), a per installation identifier, and performance and usage metrics (such as error codes and the library version). Google states that this data is encrypted in transit and is not transferred to third parties (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).

Crash reports and usage statistics

We use Google Firebase to fix crashes and to understand which features are used. Neither ever includes what you scanned or created, web addresses, Wi-Fi passwords or contact details.

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). When the app crashes, it sends the technical details of the crash (the error's stack trace and the app's state at that moment), details about the device (such as model and Android version) and a random identifier for this installation of the app. This is used only to find and fix crashes.
  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase). Only in regions where the consent message reports that no consent is needed, the app sends anonymous events about which features are used (for example that a scan was completed or a code was created, never its content), a random app instance identifier and an approximate location derived from your IP address. Where consent is required, usage statistics are not collected at all. Analytics does not collect your advertising ID.

Firebase processes this data for us as a service provider, encrypts it in transit and does not transfer it to third parties except the service providers that help Google run Firebase (https://firebase.google.com/docs/android/play-data-disclosure).

What we do not collect

Peek QR has no account. It never asks for your name, email address or phone number, and it does not read your precise location, your address book or your photo library. The only location related data is the approximate location that Google derives from your IP address for ads and, where allowed, usage statistics, as described above.

Children

Peek QR is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Clear history: Settings, Clear history, or delete items one by one in History.
  • Ad choices: Settings, Ad privacy choices.
  • Everything the app stores on your phone is removed when you uninstall it.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics are kept by Google Firebase under its retention rules. To ask us to delete them, email us at the address below; since we hold no name or email, tell us roughly when you used the app and on which phone model.
  • For data held by Google for ads, use Google's controls described in its privacy policy.
