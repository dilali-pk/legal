What Serial Hunt does and does not do

  • Serial Hunt reads the serial number on a banknote and tells you if it matches a pattern that collectors look for, such as a star note, radar, repeater, solid, ladder, binary or low serial.
  • Text recognition can misread a serial, for example in poor light or on a worn note. Check the serial with your own eyes before you keep, buy, sell or spend a note because of a result.
  • Serial Hunt does not grade notes, check that a note is genuine, or tell you what a note will sell for. Rarity notes in the app are general guidance only. What a note is worth depends on its condition and on collector demand.
  • Serial Hunt is an independent app and is not affiliated with the US Treasury, the Bureau of Engraving and Printing, the Federal Reserve or the State Bank of Pakistan.
