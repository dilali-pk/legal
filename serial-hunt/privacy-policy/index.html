Serial Hunt privacy policy

Effective 1 November 2026. Serial Hunt is made by Dil Ali, an independent developer. Contact: dilalipk@gmail.com

The short version

  • Serial Hunt has no accounts and does not ask for your name or email.
  • The camera picture is read on your phone to find the serial number and is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Your saved finds and settings stay on your phone.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and the app sends crash reports and basic usage events to Google Firebase, as described below.

What stays on your phone

  • Camera frames. Serial Hunt reads the serial with Google ML Kit text recognition running on the device. Frames are processed in memory and dropped.
  • Finds you save: the serial, the patterns found, the condition you chose and the date.
  • Settings: currency, theme, whether you finished the first run, and a count of notes checked.

This data is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it.

What third party services receive

  • Google Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports: the error, the device model, the Android version and the app version. It helps fix bugs.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase receives app usage events such as "a scan locked" or "a find was saved", with an app instance identifier. Events never contain serial numbers, photos or text you type.
  • Google AdMob shows banner ads in the free version. AdMob may use the advertising ID, device information, IP address and approximate location derived from it to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other places that require it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings, Ad choices. Serial Hunt Pro shows no ads.
  • Google Play handles the Serial Hunt Pro purchase. The developer receives no card or payment details.

Google's handling of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Sharing and selling

The developer does not sell your data and does not share it with anyone beyond the services above.

Children

Serial Hunt is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

  • You can deny the camera and type serials by hand instead.
  • You can change ad consent in Settings, Ad choices, where it applies.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in Android settings.
  • To ask about data held by the services above, email dilalipk@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date.
